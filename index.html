How it works

The architecture in five lines, for the demo walkthrough.

Pipeline
transcripts become 21 timestamped chunks (one speaker turn each), indexed as TF-IDF in this page. A question retrieves the closest turns by cosine similarity, and the answer is those turns, quoted exactly.
Model choice
No generative model in the default path. At 21 chunks, deterministic retrieval is more accurate, instant, offline and explainable. A language model may reword summaries later, but never supplies facts.
Citations
Each chunk keeps its expert, speaker and timestamp. Quotes render from the chunk record, never retyped, so every claim sits next to its source.
Against invention
Four habits: answers quote instead of generate, low scores get an explicit no-evidence reply, guide answers are pinned to single chunks, and disagreements stay side by side instead of being averaged away.
From 3 to 30 calls
Same chunk schema. Trade the in-page index for embeddings plus a vector store, add timestamp alignment for raw audio, deduplicate near-identical turns, cache per-question answers, and score citation precision (share of quotes that are exact substrings) with per-question recall.
